Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Short version

GRID is designed so that its application configuration is stored locally in your browser. GRID does not require a GRID account or a GRID-operated application server for its normal configuration and form-submission workflow. Google services are contacted when a feature that requires Google is used, such as Google authentication, Sheet access, or optional Drive synchronization.

What GRID stores locally

GRID stores its configuration in browser storage. This can include the Google Form link, Google Sheet link, Form title, configured Form fields, Google Form entry IDs, default values, and report configuration.

The app also keeps local state needed for its optional synchronization workflow. Resetting GRID removes the locally stored GRID configuration on that device.

Google Form submissions

When you submit an entry from Home, the values you enter are sent to the Google Form configured by you. GRID does not operate the destination Google Form and does not control how Google stores or processes submitted Form responses.

Google Sheet access

The Report feature can read data from the Google Sheet you configure. Access requires Google authentication and the Google Sheets read-only permission used by GRID. GRID uses the Sheet data to generate the configured report in your browser.

Entering a Google Sheet link by itself does not give GRID access to the Sheet; access is requested when a Google-connected feature requires it.

Google Drive sync

Drive synchronization is optional. When you explicitly connect Google and use GRID's synchronization feature, GRID can read and write its own configuration file, grid-settings.json, in your Google Drive using Google's drive.file permission.

Your Google authentication and Drive data remain associated with your Google account. GRID does not use a separate GRID server to receive or store your Drive data.

Google authentication

Google authentication is used for features that require Google access, such as reading the linked Sheet or synchronizing GRID settings with Drive. Authentication credentials and tokens are not included in GRID's exported JSON configuration.

JSON export and import

GRID lets you export its configuration to a JSON file for backup or transfer. Google authentication credentials and tokens are not included in the exported settings.

Cookies, analytics and tracking

GRID's application code does not include an application analytics or advertising system. The application does not require cookies for its local configuration workflow. Third-party Google services used through authentication and APIs may have their own technologies, policies, and data practices.

Offline and PWA behavior

GRID can be installed as a Progressive Web App. Its service worker provides the application shell for offline use. Google-connected features naturally require the relevant Google service and network access.

Your control

You control the Google Form, Google Sheet, Google account, and Drive configuration that you connect to GRID. You can export your GRID configuration, import a backup, manage Google access through your Google account, or use Settings → Sync → Reset app data to clear GRID's locally stored configuration on the current device.

Sharing and self-hosting

A separate hosted or forked copy of GRID can have its own Google OAuth configuration and its own Form, Sheet, and Drive settings. Operators of separate deployments are responsible for configuring their own Google services and for complying with applicable privacy and legal requirements.

Questions

Questions about GRID's privacy behavior can be raised through the project's published contact or repository information.
